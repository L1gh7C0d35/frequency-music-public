Frequency — Living Worlds

Experimental product design for the real Frequency music and manifestation practice. The preview uses real original recording files where indicated; the music player’s sound-driven visuals are measured from playback via Web Audio, not pre-scripted animations. Other elements are deliberately fictional design fixtures.

01 / Home Portal

One immersive featured Artist World with an intentional listening action. Multiple worlds can be changed using the arrows or a swipe. The three real Build 48 tabs are retained.

02 / Artist World

World art continuity, real catalog names and honest first-track playback, then queue/track navigation. No invented singers, followers, endorsements or generated scores.

03 / Resonance Player

Audio-reactive sound sculpture that responds to actual original song previews when user presses Play. Audio pauses at 0:00 / state readback if it fails. Music artwork, progress, transport and queue stay coherent.

04 / Sounds

Clear split between original music and separate generated Tone Worlds. World art belongs to music; mathematical geometry belongs to generated tone presets.

05 / Phi Geometry

A visibly mathematical tone exploration using φ arrangements and a quiet user-started tone preview. This does not assert therapeutic effects of particular Hz values.

06 / 369 Ritual

Existing real practice is kept recognizable: morning 3, midday 6, evening 9. The constellation state counts are local examples, not the user’s actual practice data.

07 / Words

Incantation and fulfilled-state creative copy, with the actual incantation-to-369 handoff. Example wording is newly written concept copy.

08 / You

A calm, truthful dashboard of real destinations rather than fabricated metrics and streaks.

Safety and truth: This site is not connected to the production app, account, subscription or stored reminders. Every visual state needs native UX evaluation before implementation. No native code, TestFlight build or recordings were modified.
