The same 50-second section of Already Grateful, with different optional Frequency layers underneath it.

Use headphones. The song is intentionally identical in every version so you can judge only the extra stereo / binaural layer. These are sound-design experiments, not guaranteed neurological or therapeutic effects.

Warm 220 Hz tone moving slowly between the ears on an 8-second orbit. No binaural difference — just spatial motion.

STRONGER LEFT ↔ RIGHT

The same warm tone with a much clearer two-second side-to-side cycle. This is simple stereo motion, not an EMDR treatment.

3

BINAURAL + MOTION

220 / 226 Hz stereo pair for a 6 Hz binaural interval, with a gentler left-right drift layered over it.

4

Phi Orbit 432

GOLDEN RATIO

432 Hz moving between the ears on a φ-inspired ~6.85-second orbit. Mathematical sound design rather than a brain-state claim.

5

Gamma Pulse 40

RHYTHMIC

A quiet 240 Hz carrier amplitude-modulated at 40 Hz. This one is centered and speaker-friendly rather than left-right.
